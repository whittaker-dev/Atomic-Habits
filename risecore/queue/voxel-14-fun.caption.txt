Đi tiệc về mà nói “The party was funny” là người ta tưởng tiệc… hài lắm đó 😂

❌ The party was funny — = Bữa tiệc buồn cười
✅ It was fun — Vui lắm
✅ It was so much fun — Vui cực kỳ
✅ It was a blast — Vui banh nóc

🧠 Đố nhanh: It was fun hay It was funny? Xem tới cuối để biết đáp án!
