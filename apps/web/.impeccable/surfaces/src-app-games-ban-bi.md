# Bắn Bi Bá Khí (/games/ban-bi)

Scope: home (name, board choice, leaderboard), full-screen arena (2D bubble board beside a 3D fighter), result. Mode: operate, with a showpiece fighter panel. Phone and desktop.
Audience/job: one player races to clear a fixed bubble board as fast as possible; bursting quickly charges the fighter through six forms. Times are compared on a per-board top 10 stored on the device.
Constraints: original character only (no existing franchise look); three.js and its bundled examples only, no new dependency; must look unlike the other games.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A dusk martial-arts arena where every burst feeds the fighter's ki; the board is a dark glass scoreboard, the fighter erupts in coloured flame.
OWN-WORLD: Ink-violet night #0a0814, glass panels #151127 with #3a2f63 lines, ki cyan #38e8ff, rage pink #ff4d8d, six form colours. Chakra Petch uppercase + Saira. Cut-corner scoreboard buttons, six-segment ki bar.
STORY: Countdown, aim and shoot, clusters burst, the fighter powers up with a flash and shockwave, the clock stops when the board is clear, the time lands on the leaderboard.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: board column left, fighter panel right with form name and ki bar. Phone: fighter band on top, timer row, board fitting the rest.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
